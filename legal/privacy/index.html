Privacy Policy

What we collect (next to nothing) and why.

Effective: 29 September 2026

The short version

Stux.Group Services is a static index of Stux.Group's services. It has no accounts, forms, analytics, tracking or advertising, and it collects no personal data about you.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its domain is managed by Cloudflare, which may also proxy requests to it. They may process your IP address and browser details to deliver the site and protect it from abuse, under GitHub's privacy statement and Cloudflare's privacy policy. We don't receive or keep those logs.

What the page loads

Fonts and service icons are served from this site. Two things load from elsewhere, both run by Stux.Group:

Live status comes from raw.githubusercontent.com (GitHub), where status.stux.group keeps its data. Seasonal overlays come from SeasonalOverlaysLibrary on stuxapis.net, from StuxAPIs. Neither request carries cookies or anything that identifies you.

Browser storage

After the seasonal overlay plays, the site remembers that for the rest of your visit (in sessionStorage) so it doesn't replay on every page. It's cleared when you close the tab and never leaves your device.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you can ask what personal data we hold about you. For this site the answer is none, but you're welcome to ask at legal@stux.group.
